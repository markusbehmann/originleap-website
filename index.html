Questions

Before you ask

What happens if I miss a day?

Depends how much you miss. Hit at least one of your seven habits and your streak survives — that's the whole design. The only day that resets you is one where you hit zero of seven.

Do I need an Apple Watch or fitness tracker?

No. OriginLeap reads whatever's already in Apple Health — from your iPhone, a watch, or any other app that writes to Health — so steps, workouts, sleep, and water can fill in automatically. If you don't have a wearable, you can always log things by hand instead.

What if I'm sick or injured?

Mark the day as a recovery day and it's excluded from the reset rule entirely. That's the one rule 75 Hard doesn't have, and it's usually the difference between quitting in week two and actually finishing day 75.

Is there an account, and where does my data go?

No account, no login, no server. OriginLeap stores your habit data, photos, and Health data on your device — we never see it. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Which diet does OriginLeap use?

Whichever one you pick. Intermittent fasting, low-carb, keto, Mediterranean, or something you wrote yourself — OriginLeap tracks whether you stuck to your own plan, not which plan you chose. The largest head-to-head diet trial to date found almost no difference in outcomes between low-fat and low-carb; consistency was what predicted success.

Is there an Android version?

Not currently — OriginLeap is available for iPhone on the Apple App Store.
