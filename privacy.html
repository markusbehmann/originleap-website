Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026
The short version OriginLeap doesn't have a server. Your habit data, your name, and your photo stay on your phone — we never see them. If you connect Apple Health, that data stays local too, is read-only, and is used solely to power the app's own features. This website loads no third-party trackers, ads, or even fonts from anyone else's servers — everything is served directly from ours.

1. Who We Are

OriginLeap is operated by Markus Behmann, based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. You can reach us at privacy@originleap.com. Full details are in our Impressum. The app is distributed on the Apple App Store by aLIVE-Service GmbH, which acts only as app store distributor — it doesn't process your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

  • Profile information (name, photo) — stored only on your device.
  • Habit log entries (workout minutes, steps, water, sleep, language-learning minutes, diet status, notes) — stored only on your device.
  • Apple Health data, if you grant access: step count, workout duration, and sleep data. This is read-only, stored only on your device, and used solely to pre-fill your daily log.

3. How We Use Information

  • On-device data powers the app's own features — tracking, streaks, and stats — and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • Health data is used only to reduce manual entry. It is never shared, sold, or used for advertising or marketing.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where any processing described in this policy counts as processing "by us" under the GDPR, we rely on: performance of a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — providing the features of the app and website you use; legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — keeping the app and website secure and working correctly; and, for Apple Health access specifically, your explicit consent given through iOS's own permission prompt (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time as described below.

5. Data Storage & Security

OriginLeap currently has no backend server — there is nowhere for your habit data to go but your own device. Locally stored data is protected by your device's standard security (passcode, encryption). Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

6. Apple Health Data

  • Access is read-only — OriginLeap never writes to your Health data.
  • Never used for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • Never shared with third parties.
  • You can revoke access at any time via Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → OriginLeap, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

7. Hosting of This Website

originleap.com is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. Visiting it causes standard technical log data — your IP address, browser type, and the page requested — to be processed by Cloudflare in order to deliver the site and keep it secure (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This may involve processing outside the EU/EEA, safeguarded by Cloudflare's data processing terms and standard contractual clauses. We don't load fonts, analytics, or any other resources from third-party servers on this website — everything, including the typeface, is served from our own hosting.

8. Local Storage

This website remembers a single preference — whether you're using light or dark mode — in your browser's local storage. This is strictly necessary for a feature you explicitly use (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG) and doesn't require consent. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and there are no third-party trackers on this site.

9. Third-Party Services

OriginLeap does not currently use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date.

10. If You Email Us

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and message to respond to you (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) GDPR). We keep that correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up.

11. Data Retention & Deletion

Locally stored data persists until you delete it yourself, either through the app's "Erase all data" option or by deleting the app.

12. Children's Privacy

OriginLeap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Your Rights

Because your habit and health data never leaves your device, we hold no copy of it to access, export, or delete on your behalf — you control it entirely through the app itself. For anything we do hold (like an email you sent us), you have the right, under the GDPR, to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of that data, to object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. To exercise any of these, contact privacy@originleap.com.

If you're in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your own country of residence.

14. International Use

OriginLeap is available worldwide. Because habit and health data stays on your device regardless of where you are, the protections in this policy apply the same way no matter which country you're in.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as OriginLeap adds features — for example, an optional cloud-sync feature in the future. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.

16. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@originleap.com.
